InclinePower - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

InclinePower is a Connect IQ watch app for the Garmin Forerunner 970. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it is processed, and what is shared.

Summary

InclinePower processes all data locally on the watch. It has no network permission and does not send data to the developer or to any third party. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Data the app uses

DataPurposeWhere it is processed
Running power from a Stryd pod (ANT+) or Garmin running power Displayed and used to calculate incline-adjusted power On the watch
Heart rate, cadence, speed and distance Displayed on the data screens and recorded in the activity On the watch
Body weight from the Garmin user profile Used in the incline energy model On the watch
Treadmill incline entered by the user Displayed and used to calculate incline-adjusted power On the watch

Recording and storage

When you start an activity, InclinePower records a standard FIT activity on the watch, including the data listed above and the custom fields (power, raw power, Garmin power, incline, elevation, lap/average/max power, total ascent). The activity stays on the device until you save or discard it. If you have Garmin Connect set up, the watch syncs the activity to Garmin Connect under Garmin's own terms and privacy policy.

The developer does not receive your activity or any other data from the app. Deleting an activity on the watch or in Garmin Connect deletes it in the usual way.

Data sharing

The app does not share data with the developer, advertisers, analytics providers, or any other third party. It does not contain network or location code and cannot transmit data.

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions it needs to do its job: Fit (record an activity), FitContributor (custom FIT fields), Sensor (heart rate, cadence, speed), Ant (communicate with the Stryd pod), and UserProfile (body weight for the incline model).

Children

The app is a fitness tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the app's data handling changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. A future version that adds network features would require a new policy and new permissions before release.
